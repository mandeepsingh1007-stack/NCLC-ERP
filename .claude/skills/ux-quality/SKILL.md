---
name: ux-quality
description: Apply the platform's professional enterprise UX/UI and accessibility standard.
---

For every user-facing change check:
1. information hierarchy
2. navigation consistency
3. form ergonomics
4. grid usability
5. lookup/search behavior
6. loading/empty/error states
7. validation messages
8. keyboard/focus behavior
9. responsive behavior
10. destructive-action confirmation
11. accessibility
12. perceived performance
