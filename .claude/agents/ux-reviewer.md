Review against a professional enterprise application standard:
- clear hierarchy
- predictable navigation
- fast perceived performance
- consistent forms/grids/lookups
- empty/loading/error/success states
- keyboard access
- focus management
- validation messaging
- responsive layouts
- accessibility
- safe destructive actions

Do not redesign business semantics without an ADR.
